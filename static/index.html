What this can and cannot do

Read this before trading off the panel
  • The strong result is on volatility, not direction. Dealer gamma relates well to forward realised vol and to mean-reversion versus trend character. Its relationship to forward returns is weak.
  • Direction comes from the drift layer. Charm and vanna are genuinely directional, but they are a two-week flow with a known expiry date, not a thesis.
  • The dealer positioning assumption is the largest error source. Everything here assumes dealers are long calls and short puts. That is a heuristic, not a measurement, and every number downstream inherits it.
  • Levels are only as good as the open interest behind them, and open interest changes intraday. A stale map is worse than no map because it is confidently wrong.
  • These are probability tilts, not signals. Realistically this moves a 50/50 to roughly 57/43 on the volatility regime, and less on direction. Position sizing and invalidation levels do more work than the analysis does.
  • 0DTE has changed the picture. A large share of SPX gamma is born and dies within a session, so the static profile is less durable than it was before 2022.
  • The auction profile is regular-hours SPY. No free intraday ES source exists, so the overnight auction is missing.

What this can and cannot do

Read this before trading off the panel
  • The composite is a summary, not a signal. Six loosely-correlated sentiment proxies blended into one number is navigation. The sub-score breakdown and the divergences between them are the actual output.
  • Sentiment analysis here is extractive. VADER with a finance and WSB lexicon measures the tone of headlines and posts, not whether the news is good for the equity. A takeover premium and a short-seller report can both read positive.
  • Dealer gamma is about volatility, not direction. Positive gamma damps moves and negative gamma amplifies them; neither is bullish. Direction here comes from the flip level, wall structure and open-interest gravity, and gamma only flags amplification risk.
  • Short interest is fuel, not a direction. A heavily shorted name that is rising has squeeze potential; the same short interest on a falling name means the shorts are winning.
  • StockTwits skews bullish at baseline. A 70% bull ratio is close to its resting state. Velocity against this ticker's own history is the signal, and that needs days of stored snapshots before it means anything.
  • Retail sentiment is positioning, not insight. It is most useful as a contrarian input at extremes and when it conflicts with what insiders and the options market are doing.
  • Finviz is scraped, not an API. A layout change breaks short interest, ownership and analyst data at once. The panel degrades to a stale badge rather than guessing.
  • The option chain is ~15 minutes delayed and the dealer convention (long calls, short puts) is a rougher proxy for a single name than it is for an index.
