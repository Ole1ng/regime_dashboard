What this can and cannot do

Read this before trading off the panel
  • The strong result is on volatility, not direction. Dealer gamma relates well to forward realised vol and to mean-reversion versus trend character. Its relationship to forward returns is weak.
  • Direction comes from the drift layer. Charm and vanna are genuinely directional, but they are a two-week flow with a known expiry date, not a thesis.
  • The dealer positioning assumption is the largest error source. Everything here assumes dealers are long calls and short puts. That is a heuristic, not a measurement, and every number downstream inherits it.
  • Levels are only as good as the open interest behind them, and open interest changes intraday. A stale map is worse than no map because it is confidently wrong.
  • These are probability tilts, not signals. Realistically this moves a 50/50 to roughly 57/43 on the volatility regime, and less on direction. Position sizing and invalidation levels do more work than the analysis does.
  • 0DTE has changed the picture. A large share of SPX gamma is born and dies within a session, so the static profile is less durable than it was before 2022.
  • The auction profile is regular-hours SPY. No free intraday ES source exists, so the overnight auction is missing.
